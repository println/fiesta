# Bookmarks

## PH-11 — Manage the bookmarks

> **As a** phone user
> **I want** to reach and manage my bookmarks, with or without a page open
> **So that** the list stays mine and is never a dead end.

> **Given** the app is open, with or without a page
> **When** bookmarks is chosen
> **Then** the bookmarks screen opens, and picking one opens that page;
> **and** each bookmark can be renamed, have its address changed, be moved in the list, or be
> deleted.

**Today.** The entry is in the overflow menu and is the one item that is there with no page
open. The list shows each bookmark with its thumbnail, or a coloured monogram when it has none,
and can be filtered by title and address. The order is the list's own, and the edits are
written when the screen is left. The bookmarks are the ones the car shows — see PH-7.

## Definitions

Not a criterion of PH-11, only what the word means here.

- **Default bookmarks.** A fresh install starts with two: Google and YouTube. They are ordinary
  bookmarks from then on, and can be deleted like any other.
