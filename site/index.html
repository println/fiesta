For sideloaders

Built for a purpose, not for everyone.

Fiesta is for people who already sideload Android Auto apps: CarStream, AAAD, AA Browser, Fermata Auto. If that sentence means nothing to you, Fiesta isn't for you yet.

Want to learn first? The AAAD project and the r/AndroidAuto community are the place to start. Fiesta has no install guide and gives no install support.

What it takes

  • Android 6.0 or later
  • Android Auto projected from your phone, not Android Automotive built into the car
  • Installing outside the Play Store, like any sideloaded Android Auto app

FAQ

Straight answers.

What is Fiesta?

An Android Auto app that puts YouTube and any website on the car screen, driven from the steering wheel buttons and Google Assistant. It plays in the background with the map on screen and picks up where it stopped when you start the car.

Is it free?

Yes. Free and open source, with no ads and no account. Donations keep it on the road, but nothing is locked behind them.

Is this the CarStream update?

It's CarStream, evolved. Fiesta picks up where CarStream stopped in 2018: it keeps the browser on the car screen and adds what CarStream never had, from steering wheel controls and voice to the Android Auto media card. The comparison above shows the rest.

What can I play?

YouTube, internet radio and any site that plays audio or video in the browser. YouTube has its own plugin, so the wheel and the media card know it inside out; other sites work through the generic one.

Will my steering wheel buttons work?

Yes. Play and pause from the same media buttons you use with any Android Auto app. Next and previous work wherever the site has them, and YouTube does.

Does it work in my car?

If your car runs Android Auto from your phone, yes: that's where Fiesta lives. Android Automotive, the Google system built into some cars that works without a phone, is a different platform and isn't supported.

Which Android version does it need?

Android 6.0 or later. And it's built for the phones you use today, on the latest Android tools. Where CarStream stalled in 2018, Fiesta keeps moving.

How do I get updates?

Every new version lands on the releases page, each with a codename from the road. Install it over the one you have.

Does it track me?

The app: no. Fiesta has no telemetry. CarStream shipped Firebase; Fiesta sends nothing, to anyone.

The sites: partly. Fiesta blocks known ad and tracking domains, on by default and adjustable per site. Sites you sign into still know it's you.

Why isn't it on Google Play?

Google only lets approved media, messaging and navigation apps onto Android Auto, and a browser for the car screen isn't one of them. Staying off the Play Store is what lets Fiesta do what Play Store car apps can't.

Does it need root?

No. Fiesta runs on a regular phone. Rooted? There's an optional unlock mode for you too.

How do I install it?

If CarStream, AA Browser, Fermata Auto or Screen2Auto already run on your Android Auto, you're one install away: Fiesta goes in the same way, no extra steps. That's why there's no install guide or install support: Fiesta is for people who've done this before.

Where do I report a bug?

On GitHub Issues. Bugs you can reproduce get fixed fastest: tell us what you did, what happened, your phone, Android version and car.
